# CareerTrack AI Knowledge Base

## Job Application Management
A job application tracker helps job seekers organize applications, monitor progress, record interview dates and schedule follow-ups.

## CV and Resume Preparation
A good CV should contain:
- Contact information
- Professional summary
- Education
- Relevant work experience
- Skills
- Professional achievements

Use measurable achievements where possible. Tailor your CV to the requirements of each job.

## Interview Preparation
Common interview questions include:
1. Tell us about yourself.
2. Why do you want this job?
3. What are your strengths?
4. What are your weaknesses?
5. Why should we hire you?
6. Where do you see yourself in five years?

Research the employer and practise answering questions using examples from your experience.

## Interview Tips
- Arrive on time.
- Dress professionally.
- Research the organization.
- Listen carefully before answering.
- Ask relevant questions.
- Send a professional thank-you message after the interview.

## Job Application Follow-ups
Follow up professionally after submitting an application or attending an interview. Respect the employer's stated recruitment timeline.

## Career Development
Job seekers should continuously improve their skills, update their CVs, build professional networks and seek opportunities that match their qualifications.
